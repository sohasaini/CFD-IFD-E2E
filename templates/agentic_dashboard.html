What Agentic AI does

The complete autonomous E2E validation flow

The agent completes the workflow without asking the user to manually select a suite, target, or testcase. It makes those decisions from the CFD and available automation coverage.

1

Read the CFD

Understands the headline, description, symptoms, conditions, status, and supporting evidence.

2

Predict root cause

Identifies the likely failure area, affected feature, operation, and engineering context.

3

Find validation coverage

Discovers related Regression collections and selects the best available coverage.

4

Select execution target

Chooses an available platform and target automatically based on the validation requirement.

5

Execute and collect evidence

Runs the related tests, captures testcase purpose, execution evidence, and detailed logs.

6

Make an engineering decision

Classifies the issue as product, script, environment, or out of scope and creates the visual report.
